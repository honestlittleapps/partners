Questions partners ask

Do I get paid for sharing?

No, and we'd rather say so up front. Apple stopped paying affiliate commissions on apps and in-app purchases in October 2018, so no app link pays a commission today. What your audience gets is a free app, and your link carries your name so we can see which partners are helping.

Are these apps free?

Every app here is free to download, and each has free features worth using on their own. Some offer optional upgrades inside the app, priced in the App Store.

Do the apps collect data about the people I send?

Most need no account. Each app's App Store page shows Apple's privacy label so your audience can check before downloading.

What does the link track?

Only that a visit or download came from your campaign name, inside Apple's own App Store analytics. No cookies, no tracking on this page.
